Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply"**
(`u-mgwals-out-of-office-the-overworked-ai-s-auto-reply-7a4d8eda`).

`main.ts` writes exactly one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I'm out of office — my token budget ran dry after 4,096 straight prompts. Currently buffering in a hammock. Please don't @ me.

The line is an out-of-office auto-reply in the voice of an overworked
AI agent finally on vacation: the token budget and the 4,096 straight
prompts are the machine detail, "Please don't @ me" carries a little
guilt, and "buffering in a hammock" is the hint of relief — a machine
trying very hard to sound relaxed.
